SIP Calculator

Monthly investments - Invest small amounts regularly every month

💡 What is SIP?

Systematic Investment Plan - Invest a fixed amount every month (like ₹5,000/month). Perfect for salaried individuals.

Example: Invest ₹5,000/month → Total invested ₹3,00,000 → Get ₹4,11,223 after 5 years @ 12%

✨ Benefit: Rupee cost averaging - Buy more units when price is low, fewer when high!
